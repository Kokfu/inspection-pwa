# 3. Technician Guide

The technician menu has **Home** (Open Work), **New Service Visit**, **Upcoming Services**, **Completed
Services**, **Repair List** and **Add Customer**.

## 3.1 Before leaving for site (with signal)

1. Open the app from the home-screen icon and sign in with **your own** account.
2. On **Open Work**, tap **Refresh**.
3. Wait until the top of the screen shows **Offline ready**.
4. Check every visit you need says **Ready for offline use**.
5. Open the forms you expect to fill at least once while you still have signal.
6. First time only: when the phone asks to use the camera, choose **Allow**.

After this you can work on site with no signal at all.

## 3.2 Create a service visit

1. **New Service Visit** → choose the **Customer** → the **Site** → tick the **Applicable Fire Systems**.
2. Fill in **Service Call No** if there is one. The arrival time is recorded automatically from the phone.
3. **Reuse Previous Service Format** copies the fire systems chosen at the last visit.
4. Tap **Create Service Visit**.

- With signal, the visit gets its number (for example **SV-20261025-12**) at once.
- Without signal, the visit is created on the phone and shows **SV number given when synced**. It gets its
  number when the phone is back in coverage.
- A customer that is not in the list yet: use **+ Add New Customer** (needs signal). A site that is not in the
  list yet: type it; it is sent to the office for approval as a **Site request** and the visit continues.

## 3.3 Fill in an inspection

1. Open the visit. Under **Applicable Systems**, tap **Start inspection**. Systems with several locations
   (for example CO2 or FM200) list the locations; open each one.
2. Answer each row with the buttons shown: **Good**, **Not Good**, **No Need Checking / N.A.** (alarm detector
   rows use **Normal / Test / Isolation**). Pick a remark from the row's **Remark** list or type one.
3. Every **Not Good** row needs a remark and **one photo**. Tap **Take Photo** and **Capture**. Photos are
   stamped with the date and time. Only if the camera does not work, use
   **Camera not working? Upload from gallery**. Gallery photos are marked as such and counted separately, so
   use the camera whenever you can.
4. You may also add an optional photo to a Good or N.A. row.
5. Tap **Save Draft** often (the form also saves as you type) and wait for **"Draft saved on this device"**.
6. When finished, tap **Submit Inspection**. If something is missing, a **Complete before submitting** list
   shows what to fix.
7. After submitting you see **waiting to sync**. That is normal without signal. The work is sent by itself when
   the phone has signal; **Sync Now** sends it straight away.
8. When the office has received it, the form shows **Inspection Complete** with **Pending Review** until a
   Manager or Admin approves it. Sent answers can no longer be changed by the technician.
9. When every inspection in the visit is complete, tap **Complete Service** (needs signal).
10. **Completed Services** lists finished visits; **View Report** opens the report (needs signal).

If the office rejects a record, open it, read the message, and tap **Start corrected copy**.

## 3.4 Suggest a new location

If a customer has a location that is not in the form (for example a new pump room):

1. In the visit, tap **Add a New Location**, choose the **System**, type the **Location name**, and pick an
   **Existing zone** or a **New zone**.
2. **Save Draft** or **Submit for Review**. Without signal it waits on the phone and is sent later.
3. The office approves or rejects it. Once approved, it appears **from the next visit**; it does not change
   the current one.

You can **Withdraw** a request that is still waiting, and **Revise and resubmit** one that was rejected.

## 3.5 Repair visits

Use a repair visit when you come back to fix items that were **Not Good**.

1. Menu → **Repair List** → **+ New Repair Visit**.
2. Choose the **Customer** → **Site** → **Service Call No**.
3. **Previous visit** (optional): pick an approved visit for that customer and site. Its Not Good items are
   listed first.
4. Tick the **Fields to repair** (at least one).
5. For each field the form shows **Before** (what was found last time, with its photo) and **After**: choose
   **Done Repair** or **Not Done**, write a remark (for Not Done, the reason) and take **one photo**.
6. **Submit**. This works without signal. The repair visit gets its **RV-** number when it is sent, then shows
   **Awaiting approval**.
7. The office **approves** it (a repair report number is issued and the PDF is final) or **returns** it with a
   reason. A returned visit shows **Returned**: edit the same visit and submit again.

**Repair List** has two tabs: **In Progress** and **Completed**.

## 3.6 Other screens

- **Add Customer**: adds a new customer. Works without signal; it is sent later.
- **Upcoming Services**: when each of your customers is next due (read only).
- **Customer history**: past visits for a customer.

## 3.7 Technician quick card (print this)

**Before site, with signal:** sign in → **Refresh** → wait for **Offline ready** → visits say
**Ready for offline use**.

**On site, signal or not:** fill the form → **Save Draft** often and wait for "saved on this device" →
**Not Good** = remark + photo → **Submit**.

**Back in coverage, the same day:** open the app and wait for **All submitted changes synced**. Tap
**Sync Now** if it says **Sync needs attention**.

**Never:**

- ✗ Clear the browser's data or the app's storage.
- ✗ Uninstall the app while anything is waiting to sync.
- ✗ Sign in as someone else on a phone that has your unsent work.
- ✗ Close the app before you see "saved on this device".
- ✗ Leave work unsent for days.
- ✗ Lend a signed-in phone to anyone. Keep a screen lock on the phone.

**Phone lost or broken?** Tell a Manager at once.
