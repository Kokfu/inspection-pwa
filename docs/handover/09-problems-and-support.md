# 9. Problems and Support

## 9.1 Technicians

| You see | Do this |
|---|---|
| **Offline** or **Server unreachable** at the top, but you have signal | Keep working; everything is saved on the phone. Tell the office the system may be down. |
| **Sync needs attention** | Tap **Sync Now** when you have signal. If it stays, open the item, read the message, and tell a Manager the visit number and the message. **Do not reinstall or clear data.** |
| A visit or form says it is not available offline | Connect, open it once, and try again. Do not create the visit again. |
| "Draft save" or photo storage error | Keep the app open, free some space on the phone, try again. Do not clear app data. |
| Camera does not open | Phone settings → Apps → Chrome (or the app) → Permissions → Camera → **Allow**. Or use **Camera not working? Upload from gallery**. |
| Cannot sign in | Check the username (capital letters matter) and the card you picked. A Manager can set a new password. |
| "This signed-in account does not have Manager access" | You picked the wrong card. Go back and pick your own role. |

## 9.2 Managers and Admins

| You see | Do this |
|---|---|
| The app address does not open for anyone | Check the office computer: on, signed in, Docker Desktop running? Wait 3 minutes after starting. Still down → call support. |
| An Admin cannot see a visit | The Admin is not assigned to that technician (chapter 2.2). |
| **Download PDF** is not available | The report is not approved yet, or the visit has corrections (chapter 8). |
| A reminder e-mail failed | Check the delivery history (chapter 4.9), fix the Gmail app password or internet, and contact the person yourself. |
| A Manager forgot their password | Call support (chapter 2.6). |
| The weekly backup check shows **BACKUP FAILED** | Chapter 6.6. |

## 9.3 Calling support

Tell support:

- what you were doing, and the exact message on the screen (a photo of the screen helps);
- the visit number (**SV-…** or **RV-…**), if there is one;
- the **Support build** shown at the top of the app;
- who was signed in.

**Never** send passwords, the system's secret settings file, or backup files by chat or e-mail.

The support contacts are written in chapter 10.1.
