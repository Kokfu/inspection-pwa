# 2. Accounts and Passwords

## 2.1 The rules

- Nobody can sign themselves up, and there is no "forgot password" link. A Manager creates every Technician
  and Admin account and sets their passwords.
- **Manager accounts are not created or reset in the app.** This is on purpose: if someone got into one Manager
  login, they could not take over the others. New Managers and Manager passwords are handled by your support
  person on the office computer (2.6).
- Passwords must be **at least 12 characters**. A short sentence is easy to remember and hard to guess,
  for example `blue-truck-rains-at-noon`.
- Every person gets **their own** account. Never share a login: the history of who did what only means
  something if one account is one person.
- Keep **at least two Managers**, held by two different people, so one forgotten password never locks the
  company out.

## 2.2 Add a Technician or Admin (Manager)

You must be signed in on the **Manager** card and be online.

1. Open **Technician List** from the menu.
2. Under **Add Technician**, fill in **Username**, **Person's name** and **Password** (12+ characters).
3. Choose the **Role**: **Technician** or **Admin (review only)**.
4. Tap **Add Technician** (the button says **Add Admin** when that role is chosen).
5. Tell the person their username and password **in person or by phone call**, not in a group chat.

### Which technicians an Admin sees

An Admin only sees the work of the technicians assigned to them. Under each technician, tick the
**Assigned Admins** boxes. If an Admin says "I cannot see a visit", this is almost always why.

## 2.3 Forgotten password (Technician or Admin)

1. **Technician List** → find the person (**Filter by username**) → **Set new password**.
2. Type the new password twice → **Set password**.
3. The person is signed out on every phone and computer and signs in again with the new password.
   Work they already sent is safe. Work still waiting on their phone is sent after they sign in again as the
   **same user**.

## 2.4 Someone leaves, or is away for a long time

- **Deactivate** (Technician List): the person can no longer sign in and is signed out everywhere. All their
  past work stays in the records. Do this on their last day.
- **Reactivate**: tick **Show deactivated staff**, then **Reactivate** next to the person. They can sign in
  again with their old password. Set a new password if the old one may be known to others.
- **Permanently remove** (Technicians only, after deactivating): tick **Show deactivated staff**, tap
  **Permanently remove** and type the username to confirm. An account that never did any work is deleted; an
  account with past work is hidden from lists but its history is kept.

Admins can be deactivated and reactivated, but not permanently removed.

## 2.5 Change a person's name

Edit **Person's name** next to the account and tap **Save name**.

## 2.6 Manager accounts: ask your support person

These are done on the office computer by the support person named in chapter 10:

| You need | Tell support |
|---|---|
| A new Manager | The person's full name and the username they want. They choose their own password at the office computer. |
| A Manager forgot their password | The username. Support sets a new password with the Manager present. |
| A Manager leaves | The username, and which other Manager stays. Support stops the account and keeps its history. |
| Nobody remembers the Manager username | Support can look it up on the office computer. |

## 2.7 Quick reference

| Task | Where | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Add a Technician or Admin | Technician List → Add Technician | Manager |
| Set a new password for a Technician or Admin | Technician List → Set new password | Manager |
| Deactivate / Reactivate | Technician List | Manager |
| Permanently remove a Technician | Technician List → Show deactivated staff → Permanently remove | Manager |
| Assign Admins to a Technician | Technician List → Assigned Admins | Manager |
| New Manager, Manager password, Manager leaving | Office computer | Support person |
