# 1. Welcome

## 1.1 What the system does

The MFE Inspection system replaces the paper service reports.

1. In the office, a **Manager** sets up each **customer**, their **sites**, and which **fire systems** are
   serviced there (for example Automatic Sprinkler, Hose Reel, Hydrant, Fire Alarm, FM200, CO2, Dry/Wet Riser,
   Portable Fire Extinguisher, Fire Rated Roller Shutter).
2. On site, a **Technician** opens a **service visit** on their phone, fills in one form per fire system
   (Good / Not Good, remarks, photos) and submits it. **This works even with no signal.**
3. When the phone has signal again, the work is sent to the office automatically.
4. A Manager or Admin checks the work and **approves the report**. The approved report can be downloaded as a
   PDF on the company letterhead.
5. If something needed fixing, a technician later records a **repair visit** with before-and-after photos,
   which is also approved in the office.
6. The day before a customer's next service is due, the system can **e-mail a reminder** to chosen people.

## 1.2 The app address

**https://app.mfets.com**

Use this address on phones and on office computers. There is nothing to download from an app store.

## 1.3 The three kinds of account

When you open the app you first choose a card. Pick the card that matches **your own** account.

| Card | Who | What they can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Manager** | Owner and office managers | Everything: staff accounts, customers, services, settings, e-mail reminders, approvals, corrections |
| **Admin** | Senior staff who check reports | Review and approve reports, repair visits and requests, and correct records, for the technicians assigned to them. Cannot manage staff or settings. |
| **Technician** | Field staff | Create visits, fill in forms, take photos, submit work, record repair visits |

Picking the wrong card does not give anyone extra rights. A Technician who taps **Manager** is told the account
does not have Manager access.

## 1.4 Signing in and out

1. Open **https://app.mfets.com**.
2. Tap your card: **Technician**, **Manager** or **Admin**.
3. Type your own **Username** and **Password** and tap **Sign in**. Usernames are case-sensitive
   (`Ali` and `ali` are different).
4. Use **Menu** (phone) or the menu on the left (computer) to move around.
5. **Sign out** is at the top right. If a technician still has work waiting to be sent, the app lists it and
   asks "Sign out anyway?". The work stays on the phone and is sent after the **same person** signs in again.
   It is better to wait until it has been sent.

You stay signed in for about 12 hours, then you are asked to sign in again.

## 1.5 Put the app on the phone's home screen (technicians)

Do this once, while connected:

- **Android (Chrome):** open the app address → menu **⋮** → **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the app address → **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**.

Then always open the app from the new icon. It opens faster, works better without signal, and its saved work
is less likely to be cleared by the phone.
