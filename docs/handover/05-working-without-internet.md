# 5. Working Without Internet

## 5.1 The rule in one sentence

**No signal stops sending, not working.** Typing, saving drafts, the camera and submitting always work. Everything
a technician enters is saved on the phone first, and sent to the office when there is signal.

## 5.2 Where the work is kept

| Where | What | Safe from |
|---|---|---|
| **The phone** | Drafts, submitted work not yet sent, photos not yet sent, downloaded customer lists and forms | Closing the app, force-closing, restarting the phone, no signal, the office computer being off. **Not** safe from clearing the browser's or app's data, or uninstalling the app. |
| **The office computer** | The official copy of everything that has been received: customers, visits, results, photos, reports, accounts | Restarts and power cuts. Protected by the daily backup (chapter 6). |

Until the office has received a piece of work, the phone holds the **only** copy. That is why technicians send
their work the same day.

## 5.3 What the status words mean

Each piece of work has a status:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Still being filled in. Only on this phone. Not sent. |
| **Pending** / **waiting to sync** | Submitted and saved on the phone, waiting for signal |
| **Syncing** | Being sent now |
| **Synced** | The office has received **this exact record** |
| **Failed** | The office could not accept it. The work is still on the phone. Open it and read the message. |
| **Conflict** | The customer's setup changed in the office (for example a site was removed). The work is still on the phone; ask a Manager. |

The phone only shows **Synced** when the office confirms it has that exact record. Sending the same work twice
never creates a duplicate.

The top of the screen sums it up:

| You see | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Online** | Connected to the office |
| **Offline** | No signal. Prepared work still works |
| **Server unreachable** | There is internet, but the office computer is not answering. Prepared work still works |
| **Preparing offline use** | Still getting ready for offline use. Stay connected |
| **Offline ready** | The app will open and work without signal |
| **N inspections waiting to sync** | Work on this phone not yet received by the office |
| **Sync needs attention** | Something failed. Open it and read the message |
| **All submitted changes synced** | Everything submitted has been received. (Unsubmitted drafts are not counted.) |

## 5.4 What works without signal

| Task | Without signal |
|---|---|
| Open the app (if it was opened with signal before) | Yes |
| Sign in for the first time | **No.** Sign in once with signal |
| Fill in forms, save drafts, take photos | Yes |
| Submit an inspection | Yes, it is sent later |
| Create a visit for a customer and site already on the phone | Yes, its SV number arrives later |
| Create a visit for a brand-new customer or brand-new site | **No** |
| Add Customer | Yes, it is sent later |
| Suggest a new location | Yes, it is sent later |
| Repair visit (if the customer was opened once with signal) | Yes |
| Complete Service, view a report, download a PDF | **No** |
| All Manager and Admin screens | **No** |

## 5.5 When work is sent

Automatically when the app opens, when signal comes back, when the app comes back to the front, and about every
minute while it is open. **Sync Now** sends straight away. A visit is always sent before its inspections, and
inspections before their photos.

## 5.6 Getting ready for a site with no signal

See the quick card in chapter 3.7. In short: with signal, **Refresh**, wait for **Offline ready**, and check
each visit says **Ready for offline use**. If a phone has not been online for about three days, some forms will
not **start** a new inspection until it reconnects (drafts already started keep working), so refresh before
travelling.

## 5.7 Updates to the app

New versions download quietly in the background. The app shows **Update now** only on a safe screen, never in
the middle of a form, and updating never deletes drafts or unsent work. Still, send all work before a planned
update.

## 5.8 When the office computer is off

- Technicians with prepared phones keep working; their work waits on the phone.
- Nobody can sign in for the first time, refresh, send work, or use Manager/Admin screens.
- E-mail reminders are not sent.
- **Nothing is lost.** Waiting work is sent when the office computer is back.

## 5.9 Things to know

- A visit created in the office while a technician's phone was offline appears on that phone only after it has
  signal again.
- The app has been tested on Android phones (Chrome and the installed app). **iPhones have not been tested**
  for working without signal; test one before relying on it in the field.
- iPhones and very full phones may delete a website's saved data if it is not used for a long time. Send your
  work the same day.
