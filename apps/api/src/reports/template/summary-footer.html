ADVICE TO CUSTOMER :-
  1. Replace fire alarm panel and pump starter batteries every 2 years or when capacity test fails, using the same ampere-hour rating.
  2. Do not block fire protection equipment, alarm devices, exit doors, fire lift lobbies, staircases or escape routes. Do not padlock exit doors.
  3. Emergency lights and KELUAR signs must be kept in working condition.
  4. Do not use fire system water (hose reel, hydrant, riser, fire tank) except for fire fighting.
  5. Do not isolate or switch off any fire alarm panel, pump set or suppression panel without informing MFE.
  6. After any renovation shut-down, ensure all control valves are returned to the OPEN position.
  7. Refill or replace any portable fire extinguisher immediately after use.
  8. Contact MFE at {{complaintHotline}} for any complaint about this service.
